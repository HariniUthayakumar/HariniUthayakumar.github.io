HARINI PORTFOLIO — QUICK EDITING GUIDE

FILES
- index.html: main portfolio homepage.
- projects.html: separate page for additional project summaries.
- assets/: profile photo and dashboard screenshots.

HOW TO PREVIEW
1. Extract the ZIP to a folder on your computer.
2. Keep index.html, projects.html, and the assets folder together.
3. Double-click index.html to open the homepage in Chrome.
4. Click “Explore other projects” to open projects.html.
5. Do not move the HTML files away from the assets folder.

DASHBOARD SCREENSHOTS
The two VAHAN screenshots appear side by side on desktop and stack on smaller screens. Click either image to open the full-size image viewer. Use the yellow “← Back to portfolio” button, click the dark background, or press Escape to return.

EDITING
- Easiest: send updated text, screenshots, and links to ChatGPT and request an updated package.
- DIY: open index.html or projects.html in Visual Studio Code or Notepad, edit the text or URL, save, and refresh Chrome.
- To replace an image, put the new image in assets/ and update the corresponding src="assets/filename.ext" in the HTML.
- Keep the folder structure intact.

LINKS
The workbook button opens your OneDrive link. Confirm sharing permissions before sharing publicly. The contact button opens Gmail compose in your browser; LinkedIn opens your profile.

PUBLISHING
This is a static website and is not public until uploaded to a hosting provider. Keep both HTML files and assets together when publishing.
